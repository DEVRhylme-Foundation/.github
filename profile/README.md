**Bridging the gap between Advanced Research and Open-Source Reality.**

DEVRhylme Foundation is a global community-driven organization dedicated to fostering innovation in the realms of **Computer Vision (CV)**, **Generative AI**, and **Web 3.0**. Our mission is to democratize access to cutting-edge technologies by building robust, production-ready open-source tools and frameworks.

## 🏛️ Our Mission
At DEVRhylme, we believe that the future of the internet is **intelligent, visual, and decentralized**. We aim to empower developers, researchers, and students to build applications that are not only technologically advanced but also ethically sound and transparent.

## 🤝 Get Involved
We are a community-first organization. Whether you are a student looking for a GSoC project or an expert looking to mentor, we welcome you!
